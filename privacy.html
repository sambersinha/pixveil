Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Welcome to Pixveil. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information related to your use of our app. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Pixveil is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary to provide our service:

  • Purchase information: When you subscribe or make a purchase, Apple handles all payment processing. We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details.
  • Favorites: Your favorited wallpapers are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.
  • Usage data: We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or behavioral tracking data.

2. In-App Purchases

Pixveil offers auto-renewable subscriptions (Weekly, Yearly) and a one-time Lifetime purchase through Apple's In-App Purchase system. All billing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID settings.

3. Wallpaper Content

Wallpapers are AI-generated images hosted on Cloudflare R2. When you browse or download wallpapers, standard network requests are made to retrieve images. No personally identifiable information is sent with these requests.

4. Photos Access

Pixveil requests access to your Photo Library only when you choose to save a wallpaper. We request the minimum permission required ("Add Photos Only") and do not read, access, or upload any existing photos from your library.

5. Third-Party Services

Pixveil uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple StoreKit: For in-app purchases and subscription management.
  • Cloudflare R2: For hosting and delivering wallpaper images via CDN.
  • OpenAI DALL-E: Used server-side to generate wallpaper images. No user data is sent to OpenAI from the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Pixveil is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is minimal risk of data breach. Your favorites and preferences are stored securely on your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
